2. Why this RBAC strategy is chosen

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) is ideal when:
- Users have distinct roles (admin, manager, user)
- Permissions need to group actions (e.g., CREATE_TASK, ASSIGN_TASK)
- Role assignments change over time
- Access rules should be centralized

3. Key conceptual pieces (and why):

- Middleware / Guards
- Enforce permissions before reaching controller logic
- Decouple authorization from business logic
- Central point to reject unauthorized access
